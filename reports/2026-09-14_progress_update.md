**Protein holding at 181 g is the thing that kept this from being a real setback** — lean mass is still protected.

## 6. Actions — in order

2. **Restart the water log** — ~100 oz, one number daily.
3. **Weigh in daily** — last reading was 9/9.
4. **Hold 2,000 on the good days.** The issue isn't one bad day; it's that the good days drifted to 2,300–2,600 and removed the buffer.
